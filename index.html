Welcome to Harmony Academy

About Our School

Harmony Music Academy is a music school in Nairobi for learners of every age. Our qualified, friendly teachers use modern, learner-centred methods so that every student can discover and grow their musical talent.

🎹

Practice Studios

Quiet practice rooms with pianos, keyboards, guitars, violins and a full drum kit for every lesson.

  • ✓ One-on-one and small group lessons
  • ✓ Instruments available in class
🎤

Ensembles & Concerts

Learners play together in bands, choirs and small ensembles, and perform at our end-of-term concert.

  • ✓ Band and choir practice
  • ✓ End-of-term concert
🎼

Theory & Exams

New to music? Practise the basics for free at musictheory.net, an online resource for reading notes, rhythm and scales.

  • ✓ Reading and writing music
  • ✓ Support for graded exams
